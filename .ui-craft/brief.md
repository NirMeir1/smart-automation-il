# Design Brief — Posiblo marketing site

## 1. Product purpose
A Hebrew-first RTL website that helps Israeli businesses and individuals describe a
problem that costs them time, money or energy, and request a free discovery meeting
with an independent technology problem-solving studio.

## 2. Primary user
An Israeli small-business owner or self-employed person, or an individual entrepreneur
with a product idea, who knows what is slowing them down but not what technology
would fix it; reading in Hebrew, often on a phone, deciding whether this person is
worth a first conversation.

## 3. Principles (ranked; higher wins in conflict)
1. **Only true things.** No invented logos, numbers, testimonials, credentials or stock
   faces posing as the operator. An unverified fact stays out or stays a visible placeholder.
2. **Problem first, technology second.** Every page and section opens with the visitor's
   situation; capability lists and tool names come after, smaller.
3. **Restraint over hype.** "Not everything needs AI." No future-of-AI visuals, glowing
   gradients or tech spectacle; the design is calm, practical and direct like the voice.
4. **Two equal doors.** "לעסקים" and "לפרטיים וליזמים" get equal visual weight; neither
   is a footnote to the other.
5. **One next step, no pressure.** The discovery meeting (לקביעת פגישת היכרות) is the
   single dominant CTA, framed as understanding fit, not selling.

## 4. Success metric
A visitor finds a situation they recognize and reaches /contact through a meeting CTA,
and submits the form without being asked to name a technology.

## 5. Out of scope
- Does not offer laptop repair, help-desk or general IT support, or imply it
- Does not show portfolio, case studies or metrics that are not verified
- Does not promise instant calendar booking (meeting CTAs go to the contact form)
- Does not add invisible tracking or analytics that are not approved
- Does not paraphrase approved Part J Hebrew copy
